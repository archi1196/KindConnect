ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Digital Community Welfare Management System

KindConnect is a student-developed web project created for our societal internship with Hanuman Kind Foundation, Surat. It provides one simple place to record community activities such as Annadaan food distribution, tree plantation, bird feeding, animal welfare, blood donation awareness, and community surveys. The dashboard helps users track activity dates and progress, while the volunteer form collects joining details. Records are saved locally in a database and can be downloaded as an Excel workbook for reporting and review.
